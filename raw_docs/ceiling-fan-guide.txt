## What it is: The Always-On Appliance

In most Indian homes the ceiling fan is the longest-running appliance after the refrigerator. In summer, a single fan can run 12 to 14 hours a day. Small differences in watts therefore add up to real money on your bill.

```
Ceiling hook ──► Down rod ──► Motor ──► Blades ──► Air delivery
                                │
                          Regulator / Remote
```

There are two motor types:
- **Regular (induction) fan:** about 50 to 80 W. Low price, simple, easy to repair.
- **BLDC (brushless DC) fan:** about 25 to 35 W. Higher price, remote control, quieter, and much more efficient.

---

## How it works: Why BLDC Uses Less Power

### Regular induction motor
The motor is powered directly from the 230 V AC supply. A large part of the electricity turns into heat inside the copper windings, and the fan speed is reduced by a regulator that wastes some power.

### BLDC motor
A small electronic driver inside the fan converts AC to DC and switches the motor windings electronically. There are no brushes to wear out.
- Far less heat loss, so the same air needs fewer watts.
- Speed is set electronically, so low speeds also save power.
- Quiet running and remote or app control are common extras.

## Specs that matter: Read the Box Like an Engineer

| Spec | What to look for | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Sweep size** | 900 / 1050 / 1200 / 1400 mm | Must suit your room size. |
| **Air delivery (CMM)** | A 1200 mm fan should deliver at least 210 m³/min under the standard | The actual breeze you feel. |
| **Power consumption (W)** | 25 to 35 W for BLDC, 50 to 80 W for regular | Drives your monthly units. |
| **Service value** | Air delivery ÷ watts. Higher is better | The number behind star ratings. |
| **BEE star rating** | Check the label for the exact model | Quick efficiency summary. |
| **ISI mark** | Present on the fan, with a licence number | Basic electrical safety. |
| **Warranty and service** | 2 years or more on the product, longer on the motor, with a service centre in your city | BLDC electronics can fail, so service matters. |

## Ratings & Sizing: Stars and Service Value Explained

BEE star ratings for ceiling fans are based on **service value** (air delivered per watt):

| Rating | Service value for 1200 to 1500 mm fans (m³/min per W) |
|---|---|
| 1 Star | about 4.0 to below 4.5 |
| 5 Star | 6.0 or higher |

Under the current standard (IS 374:2019), many older fans that used to get 5 stars now rate far lower, which is why 5 stars today usually means BLDC. A fan that shows 5 stars but has a very low air delivery is not a good buy. Always check the air delivery figure as well as the wattage.

### Room size guide (rule of thumb)

| Room size | Suggested fan |
|---|---|
| Up to about 100 sq ft (small bedroom, study) | 900 to 1050 mm |
| About 100 to 150 sq ft (bedroom) | 1200 mm |
| About 150 to 250 sq ft (living room) | 1400 mm |
| Above 250 sq ft (hall) | Two fans |

### What does a BLDC fan save? (illustrative)
Assume a regular fan at 75 W, a BLDC at 30 W, and an energy price of about ₹6 per unit:
- Saving: 45 W × 8 hours × 30 days ≈ **10.8 units a month**, which is roughly ₹65 a month per fan.
- At 14 hours a day in summer: about 19 units, roughly ₹113 a month per fan.
- If the BLDC fan costs around ₹2,000 more, payback is about 18 to 30 months depending on usage. Your own tariff and hours will change this, so use the calculator.

## 5 Checks Before You Buy

### Check 1: Verify the ISI licence
Use the **BIS CARE** app to confirm the licence number on the fan is valid for ceiling fans.

### Check 2: Calculate service value yourself
Divide the air delivery (CMM) by the wattage printed on the box. If the maths does not match the star claim, ask why.

### Check 3: Match the star label to the exact model
The star label should carry the model number of the fan in the box, not a generic sticker.

### Check 4: Test for wobble and noise
At a showroom or right after installation, run the fan at all speeds. Visible wobble, humming or clicking points to poor balancing or weak bearings.

### Check 5: Confirm service
Ask where the nearest service centre is, and what the process is for a faulty remote or driver. Keep the invoice.

## How to Choose & What to Buy: Checklist for Homeowners

- [ ] **Sweep size** chosen for the room, not by habit.
- [ ] **BEE 5-star BLDC** for rooms where the fan runs 8 or more hours a day.
- [ ] **Air delivery** printed on the box and in line with the size.
- [ ] **Remote** included, with a manual override.
- [ ] **ISI mark** and a valid licence number.
- [ ] **Warranty** of at least 2 years, with a local service centre.
- [ ] **Commonly available BLDC brands in India:** Atomberg, Havells, Crompton, Orient, Usha, Bajaj, V-Guard, Polycab. Compare current models and reviews, since specs change often.

## Installation Basics

- **Clearance:** keep the blades at least about 2.4 m (8 ft) above the floor and about 0.3 m below the ceiling. Keep the blade tips well away from walls.
- **Hook:** use a fan-rated hook or box that can carry the fan's weight and its movement. A normal lamp hook is not enough.
- **Circuit:** fans and lights normally share a lighting circuit with a 6 A or 10 A Type C MCB and 1.5 sq mm wire. See the MCB guide.
- **Regulator:** leave an old regulator at full speed or bypass it when fitting a BLDC fan.

---

## 3 Common Ceiling Fan Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Buying by price alone:** the cheapest fan can cost far more in units over its life.
2. **Judging by wattage only:** a 28 W fan that moves very little air is not efficient. Check air delivery and service value together.
3. **Oversized fan in a small room:** you end up running it on low speed, where it moves air poorly and wastes its extra cost.

---

## Safety note

A falling ceiling fan can cause serious injury. Use a proper fan hook or box, have the fan fitted by a qualified electrician, and have the wiring checked by a licensed electrician before installation.
