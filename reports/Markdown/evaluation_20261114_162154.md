3. **Reliability:** A 10kA rated circuit breaker provides a higher level of reliability and assurance that it will operate correctly under fault conditions, thus protecting the electrical system and connected devices.

In summary, insisting on a 10kA breaking capacity circuit breaker is essential for ensuring safety, preventing equipment damage, and complying with electrical safety standards in domestic installations.
